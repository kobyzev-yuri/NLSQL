### Пример проверки:
**Запрос:** "Выведи список профилей компаний, имеющих соглашения"

**Сгенерированный SQL:**
```sql
SELECT 
    bu.id,
    bu.name,
    bu.inn,
    bu.kpp,
    bu.legal_address,
    bu.phone,
    bu.email,
    bu.creationdatetime,
    COUNT(ag.id) as agreements_count
FROM tbl_business_unit bu
INNER JOIN tbl_business_unit_agreements ag ON ag.docid = bu.id
WHERE bu.deleted = FALSE
GROUP BY bu.id, bu.name, bu.inn, bu.kpp, bu.legal_address, bu.phone, bu.email, bu.creationdatetime
ORDER BY bu.name
```
